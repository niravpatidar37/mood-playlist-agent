# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want VibeForge to be a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project. Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and receive technical feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Respect different backgrounds, experiences, and communication styles.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and unwanted contact.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project welcoming.
